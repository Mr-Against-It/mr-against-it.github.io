Mr Against It — Autonomous Client-Side Engineering, 3D Artist & Physical Computing Workstations

Every application in Mr Against It executes completely inside your local browser using client-side WebAssembly (OpenCASCADE), hardware-accelerated WebGL (Three.js), and deterministic geometric solvers. Privacy Statement, Cookie Notice & Terms of Use

1. Privacy Policy & Zero-Data-Transmission Guarantee

Mr Against It operates on a strict zero-server-processing model. We do not collect, upload, transmit, log, inspect, sell, or share your CAD models, geometry files, manufacturing drawings, parametric fastener selections, project parameters, or personal identity. All file parsing, tessellation, mesh repair, remeshing, nesting, 3D fastener generation, and torque calculations run strictly inside your browser's local JavaScript/WebAssembly runtime. Because your confidential engineering assets never leave your device, Mr Against It is inherently compatible with NDA-protected, proprietary, and defense/industrial workflows.

2. Cookie Policy & Ephemeral Session Architecture

This website generates zero cookies (document.cookie is never written) and does not store user behavioral profiles or persistent tracking identifiers. On every page refresh or browser restart, Mr Against It initializes a clean, stateless session in volatile memory. To preserve work across sessions, users must explicitly download/save their project files or exports locally to their own filesystem and manually open them again when resuming work.
